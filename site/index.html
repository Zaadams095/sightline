Everything in one place

Every camera at once

All your cameras live on one screen, from any brand. Tap one for full screen, zoom, and move cameras that can turn.

Find anything fast

Scroll a timeline, or just ask: “When did the package arrive?” Answered on your iPhone.

Alerts that learn

Choose the cameras and things that matter. Tap “Not important” and Sightline quiets similar alerts.

Doorbell rings

A full-screen ring with live video, on your iPhone, Lock Screen and Apple Watch. Talk back on cameras that support it.

Familiar faces

Optional. Name family members and your car, so alerts say “Dad at Front Door”. Recognition happens only on your iPhone.

Home & Away

Fewer alerts while you’re home, more while you’re out. Family check-ins let you know Mom’s day has started.

Share safely

Give family access you can limit or take back. Share a clip with neighbors with faces and plates blurred.

iPhone, iPad, Watch and TV

An iPad wall display, Apple Watch, Apple TV, widgets and Siri. In English, Spanish, French, German and Portuguese.

Frigate, Reolink, Hikvision, Annke, Dahua, Amcrest, Lorex, Swann, UniFi Protect, Blue Iris, Agent DVR, Shinobi, ZoneMinder, Scrypted, TP-Link Tapo, Wyze, eufy, EZVIZ, Imou, and any ONVIF or RTSP camera.

Some cameras need a setting turned on first; the app walks you through it. Ring, Nest, Arlo and Blink only work with their own cloud, so they can’t be shown in a private app. Details. Brand names belong to their owners; Sightline isn’t affiliated with any camera maker.

Private by design

Sightline has no account and no servers of ours. Your video goes from your cameras to your phone.

  • No sign-up, no subscription, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Camera passwords stay in your iPhone’s Keychain. Backups go to your own iCloud.
  • Away from home, use the built-in WireGuard or your own Sightline Hub.
